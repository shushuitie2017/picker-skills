## L1 · Foundations
- **Must demonstrate:** ship a clean 45–120s film; correct duration; embedded audio;
  no broken/mislabeled assets; legible on mute.
- **Gate:** one piece, composite ≥ 18/35, zero technical defects.
- **Do not regress:** technical soundness; assets verified; honest critique written.

## L2 · Motion
- **Must demonstrate:** frame-driven entrance animation (useReveal/interpolate),
  ≥2 distinct transition types, easing that doesn't read robotic.
- **Gate:** motion subscore ≥ 3.5/5 on one piece.
- **Do not regress:** no CSS-animation reliance; no first-frame "pop to final state".

## L3 · Composition
- **Must demonstrate:** compositing self-collected real imagery with cinematic camera
  (match-cut zoom / Ken-Burns), grade + vignette + grain, a bespoke score whose
  impacts sync to the cuts.
- **Gate:** real PD imagery composited with cinematic camera moves + a bespoke score
  whose impacts sync to the cuts; composite >= 24/35.
- **Do not regress:** real-asset verification (eyeball content), synced audio,
  emotional payoff present.

## L4 · Depth & Hook
- **Must demonstrate (pick ≥2 as stretches over coming cycles):**
  1. **2.5D depth** — cut a flat image into fore/mid/back layers (mask via luminance
     or a quick segmentation) and parallax them, OR add a true volumetric particle
     layer with parallax, so motion no longer reads as flat pan-zoom.
  2. **First-3s hook** — open on motion/tension, not a slow title card; title arrives
     AFTER the hook. Caption/title legible for silent autoplay.
  3. **Rhythmic editing** — cut lengths vary with the music; at least one accelerating
     montage section. Not uniform ~10s scenes.
- **Gate:** depth subscore ≥ 3.5 AND hook subscore ≥ 4 on one piece.
- **Do not regress:** L3 items.

## L5 · Narrative
- **Must demonstrate:** a genuine arc (setup→turn→payoff) where the EDIT carries
  meaning, not just captions; restraint with on-screen text; an earned ending.
- **Gate:** emotional-payoff ≥ 4 AND coherence ≥ 4, and a blind re-watch (you, next
  cycle, reading only the LEDGER summary) would still "get it".

## L6 · Originality
- **Must demonstrate:** a visual technique NOT already in TECHNIQUES.md as "proven"
  (e.g., datamosh-style transition done frame-driven, generative shader-like fields,
  kinetic typography as the lead, match-cut on SHAPE not just scale).
- **Gate:** originality ≥ 4 and the new technique promoted to TECHNIQUES "proven".

## L7 · Feedback-driven
- **Must demonstrate:** ingest real METRICS (views/likes/watch-time the user pastes),
  form a hypothesis ("hooks under 2s + vertical → more retention"), ship an A/B pair
  testing it, and let the data update the rubric weights.
- **Gate:** ≥2 metric-informed iterations that move the real number in the
  hypothesized direction (with the caveat that small samples are noise).
