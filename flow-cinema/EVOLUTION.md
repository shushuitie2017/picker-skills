# EVOLUTION — the flow-cinema capability ladder

Advance ONE level only when the gate is met with logged evidence in LEDGER. No skipping.
Each cycle must attempt ≥1 stretch toward the next level.

- **L1 — Single clean shot.** One 8s clip in the target style, no garbled text, watched
  and judged.
- **L2 — Coherent multi-shot reel.** ≥5 shots, one consistent style/grade/aspect,
  assembled in order, honest critique. Gate: a watchable 40s+ reel with no hard fail.
- **L3 — Character-consistent film.** The protagonist is CAST via 角色 and reads as the
  same person across all their shots. Gate: dimension-1 score ≥6 on a ≥10-shot film.
- **L4 — Synced post-production.** Single-voice narration + title/subtitle cards + BGM
  laid over the cut (Remotion or NLE), audio locked to shot cuts. Gate: a delivered
  film with the full post stack, not just raw clips.
- **L5 — Hook & pacing (now measurable via the engagement layer).** First-3s hook proven
  (opens on detail/conflict, not an empty wide); shot lengths varied via the 剪辑时长
  trim model (not all flat 8s); ≥1 match-cut or motivated transition; the act's theme is
  SHOWN (paired low/high angles), not just narrated. Gate: **RUBRIC dim 6 (ENGAGEMENT)
  ≥6/8 AND dims 4+5 ≥10 combined**, with the act passing the CINEMATIC-CRAFT §1 gate (no
  28/48 engagement cap). Read `references/CINEMATIC-CRAFT.md` + `references/FILM-DECONSTRUCTION.md`.
- **L6 — Frame-control & continuity.** Use 帧 (first/last-frame) refs to chain shots so
  motion/composition carries across cuts; consistent props/locations. Gate: ≥2 shot
  pairs with deliberate continuity.
- **L7 — Repeatable series.** A reusable cast + world bible so new episodes stay
  on-model with minimal re-casting. Gate: a 2nd film reusing a saved character.
